Recommendation 1

Improve asset productivity before major expansion

PortCo should prioritise digital coordination, yard and gate optimisation, reefer expansion and targeted crane upgrades, while deferring major berth and channel works until demand triggers are proven.

Near-term package A$137m first 3 years
Total first-phase investment A$137m A$119m capex plus A$18m rail opex
EBITDA uplift A$22-34m annual impact by year 3
Maintenance savings A$4-7m annual reduction from crane and reefer reliability
Payback signal ~4 years before committing to major berth/channel works
1

Priority 1

Integrated terminal control system

Connect vessels, yard, cranes, gates, reefers, trucks and rail in real time so cargo moves faster through the same footprint.

  • Truck turnaround: 58 minutes to 40-45 minutes
  • Missed or rescheduled pickups: 18% to below 10%
  • Peak gate overload: 30% to below 10-15%
  • Average vessel turnaround: 48.5 hours to 32-36 hours
  • Average container dwell: 4.8 days to approximately 3 days
2

Priority 2

Yard redesign and extended gate operations

Reconfigure the terminal yard into dedicated import, export, reefer, empty and fast-pickup zones, then extend gate hours before building new land.

Import zone Fast pickup lane Export zone Reefer zone Inspection and customs Empty container zone
  • Usable yard capacity: 4% to 15%
  • Faster container retrieval and fewer reshuffles
  • Lower truck queues, road congestion and land expansion pressure
3

Priority 3

Add 600-800 reefer plugs

Grow capacity from about 2,400 plugs to 3,000-3,200 smart monitored plugs for the fastest-growing and highest-value cargo segment.

Current2,400 plugs
to
Future3,000-3,200 plugs
  • Reefer capacity uplift: 25% to 33%
  • Reduces immediate unpowered-reefer risk
  • Supports December-March agricultural export peak
  • Enables energy-based pricing and AI monitoring
4

Priority 4

Targeted crane upgrades

Retrofit three cranes with insufficient outreach, replace two high-risk cranes, and refurbish the remaining fleet.

  • Faster handling of 5,400-6,500 TEU vessels
  • Lower vessel turnaround and berth occupancy
  • Less dependence on limited-capability cranes
  • Illustrative maintenance saving target: A$3.9-7.8m per year
5

Priority 5

Defer major berth and channel works

Start feasibility, design and approvals now, but proceed with major civil works only when clear demand and operating triggers are met.

Berth strengthening or selective extension Major berth expansion Channel deepening Channel feasibility and approvals
  • Preserves capital for higher-return near-term actions
  • Keeps berth and channel options ready
  • Avoids premature, hard-to-reverse investment
